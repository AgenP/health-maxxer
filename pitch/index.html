Health-Maxxing (Health OS)

Plastic and chemical exposure can cause massive damage to our health. Snap a quick pic of what you're eating or drinking, or the water source it came from, and in ~5s get an estimate of plastic exposure and a recommendation for safer alternatives.

The first live module of a personal Health OS. Bloodwork, sleep, and gym & nutrition come next.

The problem

You make exposure decisions in seconds, with no information.

Pain

Yesterday, in my NYC hotel room, I looked at the tap and had one thought: "uncertainty about quality."

I went straight to the front desk and took two plastic bottles. No lookup. No comparison.

Why it matters

72%
of 236 everyday US foods and drinks had detectable phthalates in lab tests.

~2x
the microplastic particles in bottled water vs tap.

The data exists, but it's buried in lab reports and PDFs, not where you're choosing.

Solution

Point your phone at it. In ~5s you get:

  • a relative tier: lower, typical, higher
  • how strong the evidence is (lab-measured or inferred)
  • how it compares to official reference levels
  • a swap that's actually backed by data
Sources: PlasticList (DEHP-equivalents; 169 of 236 products had at least one sample above the limit of quantification) · Mason et al. 2018 (particles >100 µm) · Philip's own story (self-interview; the only user research so far)
